Sometimes, the `x:SetterTargetType="X"` and `Property="X"` properties can trigger errors in AXAML
for no apparent reason, even when the syntax is correct. This can happen due to invalid caches
caused by the Avalonia extension for Visual Studio. To resolve this, follow these steps:

1. Delete the "bin" and "obj" folders from the project folders that do not have the ".Desktop"
   suffix. For example:
   - Motoplay
   - InstallerMotoplay
2. Ensure that the Avalonia extension is up to date in Visual Studio.
